# Lost Phone

you find a random laptop near a terminal during heavy rain.

it's still warm.

it's unlocked.

the owner is nowhere around.

so obviously, you start looking through it.

probably a terrible idea.

## before you play

**pay attention to everything.**

seriously.

this game doesn't always tell you when something is important.

a random message, timestamp, filename, old photo, deleted file, weird search, or even something that looks completely useless might make more sense later.

some clues only matter after you find something else.

some things look suspicious but aren't.

some things look normal until you notice the time.

and not everything you read is necessarily true.

## small hints

* check **timestamps**, not just what the file says.
* compare what one person says with what **other people** say.
* if two clues seem connected, make sure they actually are.
* old files can matter just as much as recent ones.
* archived chats and hidden files exist for a reason.
* photos don't always prove what you think they prove.
* someone's notes tell you what **they believed or felt**, not automatically what actually happened.
* don't ignore normal files. sometimes they tell you more about someone than the creepy ones do.
* the terminal around you changes as the night goes on. pay attention to that too.
* if you see `???` somewhere, you probably haven't found enough yet.
* you do **not** need to open absolutely everything, but rushing can make you miss important context.
* your choices near the end matter.
* there is more than one ending.

## one important rule

don't decide what happened too early.

you will probably make a theory.

then find something that ruins it.

that's normal.

keep looking.

## controls

click stuff.

open apps.

read messages.

check folders.

look through photos.

listen to recordings.

use the terminal status.

and when it becomes available, **check your timeline.**

that's basically it.

there are no jumpscares you need to prepare for.

just be nosy.

and maybe remember that this was somebody's laptop before it became your mystery.
